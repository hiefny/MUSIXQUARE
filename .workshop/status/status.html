How we check

Automated checks run every 15 minutes, and each bar under a service is one check from the last 24 hours. A service shows Problem only after two checks in a row fail, and Not checked when no check has finished in the last 30 minutes.

We check from our side, so a problem that appears only on some networks or devices may not show here. If something isn't working for you, email contact@musixquare.com.
