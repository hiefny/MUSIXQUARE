Typography

A face for each script, one shared line box, and the font's own spacing. A track title looks the same in Korean and English, apart from characters left to the device's fonts.

Scripts

MusicInter 음악MUSIXQUARE Hangul 音楽Noto Sans JP おんがくNoto Sans JP

Each run is drawn by its script's face. The kana marks this track title as Japanese, so its Han takes Japanese shapes too.

Faces

  • AЖΩ Inter 4 Latin, Cyrillic, Greek, Vietnamese and digits. Seven subsets, with an optical size axis from 14 to 32.
  • 한 MUSIXQUARE Hangul Hangul. Pretendard 1.309, cut into 87 subsets and renamed, as its license requires.
  • 漢あ Noto Sans KR, JP, SC, TC Han, kana and the symbols Inter lacks. Kana comes only from JP, and KR fills the 98 Hangul that Pretendard lacks.
  • ก ع א क Noto Sans Thai, Arabic, Hebrew and 8 Indic Each draws its own script, at its own metrics.

Emoji, scripts with no app face and 1,270 listed rare characters fall to the device's fonts, so they can look different between interface languages.

Line box

Ag 한 漢 あ

Ascent 1950 Baseline Descent 494

Latin, Hangul, Han and kana share one box: 1950 units above the baseline and 494 below, of 2048, with no line gap. Inter is scaled to 97.2% to match Pretendard's cap and x-height, and Noto CJK to 94% so Hangul, kana and Han sit at one size. Each face's ascent and descent overrides then put it on the box. The box is also written into the font files, for browsers that ignore the CSS overrides.

No text is tightened. Inter's optical size axis follows the type size from 14 to 32, and the font does the rest. Shown at one size, opsz 32 sets the word about 5% narrower. Only codes, PIN digits and a few small labels track wider. Codes also use tabular figures, so each digit reads on its own.

Rules
  • The field turns red only when what was typed is wrong. A connection or service problem turns only the line red.
  • Errors clear on the next keystroke.
  • Lines under a field have no full stop and put one sentence on each line. Progress lines keep "...".
  • The line follows the field: centered under a code, at the text's start under an entry.
  • Dialogs keep one line of space under the field, so nothing moves when an error appears.
  • The blue underline is the focus mark. No field draws a ring.

Iconography

Inline SVG keeps icons dependency-free and lets them inherit currentColor. Filled Material-style paths are the default; stroke or mixed construction is reserved for affordances whose shape depends on lines, such as reordering and volume waves. There is no icon font, sprite, or decorative emoji. Material paths retain their Apache License 2.0 notice.
