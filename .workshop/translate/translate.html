        4. A few translation notes

        Write for people using the app
        Keep the tone friendly and direct. Buttons should be easy to understand, even without the surrounding explanation.
        Use the right meaning
        Left and Right describe speaker positions. A chat mute limits messages; it does not mute audio. Use the same terms for the same features.
        Keep product details intact
        Keep names such as MUSIXQUARE and YouTube, variables such as {{count}}, units, and feature conditions. Report unclear source wording in your explanation.
        Clarity comes before a shorter sentence
        Shorten a phrase when it feels natural. Do not remove a useful condition just to fit a button. Length checks help reviewers; they are not a translation verdict.
